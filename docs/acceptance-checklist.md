# Acceptance checklist

Traceability checklist for the 50 original requirements in `docs/specifications/Requirements.txt`, informed by `docs/specifications/Design.txt` and `docs/data-contract.md`. Automated build, unit, and browser checks have passed for the specific cases recorded below; manual and external acceptance work remains open where marked. See the [verification record](verification.md) for commands, browser versions, measurements, and limits. Keep each status tied to its stated evidence.

Checkboxes are checked only where the recorded automated or repository evidence covers the stated criterion. A passed automated subcheck does not complete an item when manual or external work remains pending.

## Evidence limits

The [verification record](verification.md) summarizes 24 passing Node tests, the 3,525-check Colorado build, automated browser integration/scenario/fixture checks, and the measured performance profile. Automated tests, axe scans, screenshots, and network inspection document specific properties; they do not establish that first-time users succeed, that screen-reader output is understandable, or that every WCAG requirement is met. WAVE, hands-on assistive-technology testing, real-phone testing, first-time participant observation, a second-maintainer walkthrough, and production-host checks remain outstanding. Keep those claims open until their evidence is recorded.
